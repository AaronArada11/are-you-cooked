# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers practicing Python algorithm interviews, explaining their approach while coding on a laptop.

## Product Purpose

A focused problem → explanation → implementation → tests → evidence-linked review flow. The current backend records sessions; execution and AI evaluation are not yet configured.

## Brand Personality

Playful, focused, encouraging. An original steak interviewer gives the product personality without judging the candidate.

## Anti-references

No overwhelming decoration in the editor, mocking performance reactions, arbitrary scores, dashboard-first navigation, or fabricated assessment.

## Design Principles

- Protect coding focus.
- Make recording and service limitations visible.
- Separate mascot interaction states from performance.
- Ground feedback in observable session evidence.
- Keep the first experience to one curated exercise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, responsive panels, and text labels alongside all doneness colors.
